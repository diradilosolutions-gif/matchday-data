Win chances come from this site’s own model, built from each league’s results (football-data.org), and back-tested against past seasons. They’re probabilities, not guarantees. Market angles are model estimates built from those win chances and league scoring rates, not real xG or bookmaker odds, and cards aren’t modelled because no card data is available. Tap a match for goals outlook, risk factors, motivation and team news. Injury news comes from the Fantasy Premier League feed, API-Football and dated manual notes, with the source and time shown; it is not used in the win chances.
